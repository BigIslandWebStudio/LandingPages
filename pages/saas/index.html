Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of full Pro features with no credit card required. At the end, you can downgrade to Free or choose a paid plan.

Can I import from other tools?

Yes! We support one-click imports from Jira, Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Linear, and more. Your data migrates in minutes.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypt all data at rest and in transit, and offer SSO with SAML on Enterprise plans.

What's the AI automation limit?

Free plans include 10 AI actions/month. Pro includes 100. Enterprise is unlimited. Actions include auto-assignment, smart scheduling, and workflow triggers.
