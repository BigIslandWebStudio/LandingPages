Our Story

Born from a century of perfumery tradition

In the sun-drenched fields of Grasse, where generations of master perfumers have honed their craft, AURELIA was born from an obsession with the extraordinary.

Our founder, Isabelle Laurent, spent fifteen years traveling the world's most remote corners, seeking the rarest essences known to perfumery. From the night-blooming jasmine of the Himalayas to the precious oud of Cambodia's ancient forests.

Each AURELIA fragrance is a testament to patience, craftsmanship, and an unwavering commitment to capturing moments of transcendent beauty in a bottle.
